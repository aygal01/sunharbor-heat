**Sunharbor Heat** is an original open-world crime game that plays in a desktop browser from static files, no build step and no network requests. Explore the sun-soaked coastal city of Sunharbor on foot and by car: weave through traffic, steal rides, take on story missions for the dockside crews, earn cash, and outrun the Sunharbor Police Department as your wanted level climbs. Full HUD with a live minimap, combat, busted and wasted outcomes, and a save that remembers your progress.

Everything is original: the city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art and sound were created for this game.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Move with WASD or the arrow keys, Enter/F to get in and out of cars, Shift to sprint, Space to punch, the mouse to aim and click to fire once you carry a piece, M for the map legend and Esc to pause. Your progress saves automatically in the browser.

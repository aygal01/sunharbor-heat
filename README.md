**Sunharbor Heat** is an original top-down open-world crime game that runs in a desktop browser straight from static files — no build step, no network requests, nothing to install. You play Raka, a boardwalk courier in the sun-soaked coastal city of Sunharbor, and one small favour for the marina crews turns into a night of errands across five districts: downtown towers, quiet suburbs, the industrial docks, the marina, and the beach with its boardwalk and palms.

The city is alive around you. Traffic follows the road grid and brakes for jaywalkers; pedestrians stroll the sidewalks and scatter when things get loud. Any parked or slow car can be yours — hop in with F — but crimes with witnesses raise your wanted level from one to five stars, and the Sunharbor PD answers with patrol cars, road pursuit and, at high heat, officers on foot and a bust meter. Work through a four-mission story chain for Mira, Dario, Sinta and Bram, take repeatable SunPost courier runs between jobs, punch with Space, and once the story puts a pistol in your hand, aim with the mouse and click to fire. Get caught and you are busted; get flattened and you wake up at Sunharbor General — either way the heat resets and your wallet takes the hit. A full HUD keeps cash, health, wanted stars, the clock, your current objective and a live minimap on screen, all sound is synthesised in the browser, and your cash and story progress save automatically.

Everything is original: the city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art and sound were created for this game.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.

## Controls

- **WASD / arrow keys** — walk, or drive the car you are in
- **Shift** — sprint on foot
- **F or Enter** — get in or out of the nearest car (any car you can reach is fair game)
- **E** — talk to a mission giver, or take a courier run at the SunPost hub
- **Space** — punch
- **Mouse + click** — aim and fire the pistol, once the story gives you one
- **Esc** — pause

Your progress saves automatically in the browser; a saved game offers Continue on the title screen.
